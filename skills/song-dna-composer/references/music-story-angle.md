# Music Story Angle

Use this reference only when the user explicitly wants content, reel, caption, or script material about an existing song. For creating new songs, prefer `creative-dna.md`.

## Candidate Scoring

Score candidates only when comparison helps the user choose. Use a 10-point scale:

- Backstory strength
- Visual potential
- Emotional clarity
- Cultural/literary/film value
- Vietnamese audience accessibility
- Rights/sensitivity risk

Do not pretend the score is scientific; it is an editorial filter.

## Strong Content Angles

Look for:

- a song born from separation, war, migration, exile, loss, or reunion;
- a film theme that outlived the film;
- a song adapted from poetry, folklore, literature, or a historical figure;
- a cover or later performance that revived the song;
- a mismatch between surface mood and origin, such as a bright melody with a sad backstory;
- a visual symbol that can anchor AI images or video: rain, train station, old house, bridal veil, battlefield, letter, river, moon, city lights, radio, record, stage curtain.

## Reel Conversion

For short-form video, extract:

- a first-three-seconds hook;
- one central image;
- one emotional turn;
- one line of narration that makes the viewer stay;
- three to five scene beats;
- what not to show if the story is uncertain or sensitive.

Avoid overexplaining music theory unless the user's angle is production analysis.

## Fit For "Nhạc Thơ" Style Content

Prefer songs whose story can be framed with poetic restraint:

- longing rather than celebrity gossip;
- origin and atmosphere rather than listicles;
- cultural memory, old films, classic literature, or famous performance moments;
- clear visual prompts for 9:16 reels without relying on on-screen text.
