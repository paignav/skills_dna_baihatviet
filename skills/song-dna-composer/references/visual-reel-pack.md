# Visual Reel Pack

Use this reference when the user wants cover art, AI images, AI video clips, Reels, Shorts, MV concepts, storyboard prompts, or visual material to support a new song.

## Goal

Create a coherent visual identity for the song, not isolated pretty prompts. The visual concept should come from the song's emotional engine, central metaphor, hook, and target platform.

## Required Components

For a compact visual pack, include:

- **Art concept name:** a short title for the visual world.
- **Key visual:** the one image that represents the song.
- **Characters:** age range, role, emotional state, costume, pose.
- **Location:** culturally coherent setting, preferably concrete and inspectable.
- **Symbolic object:** one or two objects only, such as umbrella, rương áo cưới, chén trà, quạt, khăn hồng, hoa nhài, bến sông, trống hội.
- **Color palette:** three to five colors tied to emotion.
- **Lighting and camera:** practical visual direction, not vague beauty words.
- **Image prompt:** 9:16, no text, no watermark, no logo unless the user asks otherwise.
- **Shot list:** 5-8 second clips mapped to song sections.

## Short-Form Video Rules

For Reels and Shorts:

- Use vertical 9:16 composition.
- The first shot must read instantly within three seconds.
- Avoid on-screen text unless the user specifically wants typography.
- Keep characters and costumes consistent across prompts.
- Use repeatable visual anchors for the chorus so viewers recognize the hook.
- Give each clip a clear motion: turn, step, raise umbrella, lower gaze, sleeve sweep, lantern dim, rain ripple, camera push-in.
- Include start frame and end frame when the user will use image-to-video tools.

## Vietnamese Adaptation Rules

When adapting from Chinese or other references into Vietnamese aesthetics:

- replace generic "ancient Chinese palace" with a specific Vietnamese-coded setting when appropriate: Hue imperial corridor, Thang Long citadel-inspired gate, village communal house, old riverside house, tiled-roof courtyard, lantern festival, northern village lane, royal garden, ferry dock.
- replace overly Chinese props with Vietnamese-compatible objects when needed: non la, ao tac, ao dai co dung, khan van, quat giay, trong hoi, chum nuoc, cau da, ben song, den long.
- Do not mix every cultural sign at once. Choose one coherent world.

## Negative Prompt Defaults

Use when generating images or video:

```text
text, subtitle, watermark, logo, extra fingers, distorted hands, deformed face, duplicate people, blurry subject, modern objects, random Chinese characters, oversaturated neon, low quality
```
