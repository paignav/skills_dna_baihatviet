# Creative DNA Extraction

Use this reference when the user wants to make a new song from the study of famous songs, classic film themes, hit records, poems, literature, folklore, or story sources.

## Extract These Layers

For each reference source, identify only the layers that help create a new song:

- **Emotional engine:** what unresolved feeling powers the song?
- **Narrative pressure:** what situation makes the feeling urgent?
- **Speaker position:** who is singing to whom, from what distance?
- **Hook promise:** what simple emotional sentence does the chorus deliver?
- **Contrast:** what changes between verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge, or drop?
- **Sonic/style signature:** for songs, what instrument, rhythm, texture, or vocal color makes it recognizable? For literature, what atmosphere, imagery, voice, or symbolic system makes it memorable?
- **Memory image:** what visual object or scene sticks in the listener's mind?
- **Audience use:** karaoke, crying alone, wedding, film montage, transformation reel, dance trend, nostalgia, patriotic memory, or spiritual release.

## Convert DNA Into New Music

Never copy the surface. Convert the reference into an original brief by changing:

- era or location;
- relationship;
- object/metaphor;
- hook sentence;
- genre blend;
- vocal perspective;
- ending emotion.

Example transformation logic:

- Reference DNA: "love survives disaster and distance."
- New Vietnamese angle: "a person waits through many rainy seasons beside an old ferry, believing the other will remember the way home."
- New hook type: short vow, easy to sing, no copied lyric.

## Candidate Scoring

When choosing sources for new-song creation, score:

- Emotional power
- Hook clarity
- Adaptability to Vietnamese lyrics
- Visual metaphor strength
- Karaoke/trending potential
- Copy-risk level

Prefer sources with high emotional power and low copy-risk. A song with an iconic melody but no adaptable story may be less useful than a poem, legend, or scene with a strong emotional setup.

## Useful Deliverables

For a selected direction, produce:

- title options;
- one-sentence song premise;
- target listener/use case;
- BPM/key/vocal range suggestion when useful;
- structure map;
- chorus hook candidates;
- lyric draft or lyric skeleton;
- Suno/ACE-Step prompt;
- visual/reel concept if relevant.
